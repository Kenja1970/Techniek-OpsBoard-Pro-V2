What it does

Flow you can manage

A pull system with real WIP limits, work-item aging, and bottleneck detection — not a to-do list with columns. Moves are gated the same way whether a person drags a card or the agent proposes it.

Earned value that reconciles

CPI, SPI, EAC, and variance derived from the work items themselves, with source-system overrides when a P6 or ERMAS extract should win. Metrics are computed, never typed.

A/E financial controls

Earned multiplier and contribution margin against your target, rules of credit, change control, and funded-value exposure — the numbers a principal actually asks about.

The PM Assistant

Pick one project, several, or the whole portfolio and ask in plain language. The answer is assembled from metrics this application computed and findings it inspected, then paired with passages retrieved from your procedure library.

  • Every suggestion cites the clause behind it, or says plainly that nothing covers it.
  • Every figure is checked against the underlying evidence before it is shown.
  • Conditions your procedures do not cover are reported as a gap register.
  • It works with no AI service configured — the answer is then composed deterministically.

Your procedures are private to your account. Nothing you upload is visible to other users.
